5. Interpretation

    Total score Sn. E[Sn] = nμ and Var(Sn) = nσ2. Both grow with the number of systems: larger infrastructures give larger absolute differences between lucky and unlucky attackers, of order √n.

    Average score Sn/n (LLN). Var(Sn/n) = σ2/n → 0, so by Chebyshev P(|Sn/n − μ| > ε) ≤ σ2/(nε2) → 0: the success rate of every attacker converges in probability to μ.

    Normalized score Sn/√n (CLT). Var(Sn/√n) = σ2 for every n, the only scaling that keeps the fluctuations neither exploding nor vanishing. In the Rademacher case (μ = 0, σ = 1) Sn/√n converges in distribution to N(0,1); for Bernoulli the same holds after centring, (Sn − np)/√(np(1−p)).

    Attackers m. m does not change the law of one attacker's score. It sets how accurately we observe it (KS error of order 1/√m) and how extreme the best attacker is: the maximum of m roughly standard-normal scores grows like √(2 ln m).
